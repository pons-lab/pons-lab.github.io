Why “PONS”?

Our name stands for Physiological & On-body Neural Systems — and it is also the brainstem structure that relays signals between the brain and the body. In Latin, pons means “bridge.” True to its name, we build bridges: between brain and body signals, between humans and machines, and between everyday life and healthcare technology.

P

Physiological

We measure the body's vital signals — cardiac, respiratory, muscular, and beyond.

O

On-body

We build soft, skin-conformal wearable devices for everyday life.

N

Neural

We sense and decode brain signals, from EEG to brain–computer interfaces.

S

Systems

We integrate sensing, signal processing, and applications into complete systems.
